Opportunities

Join the group

Motivated students are always welcome. Students in the group take part in the full research cycle, from simulation and hardware to analysis and publication, and work with international collaborators.

PhD

Doctoral projects in neutrino physics and analysis, or in detector development: LGAD and diamond sensors, gaseous detectors and diagnostics.

Master's by research

Focused projects such as machine-learning analysis of neutrino events, Geant4 or TCAD simulation, or detector prototyping.

Final-year projects

Undergraduate projects for physics students at XMUM, giving a genuine first research experience with simulation, data or hardware.

Internships & visitors

Short research stays for students from other universities, when a suitable project and supervision are available.

How to apply

Getting in touch

Email mohammad.nizam@xmu.edu.my with the subject line “Prospective student – [PhD / Master's / Project / Internship]”, and include:

  • a CV and academic transcripts;
  • a short paragraph on which research theme interests you and why;
  • any programming, laboratory or research experience;
  • your intended start date.

Formal admission to postgraduate programmes is through Xiamen University Malaysia. Funded positions, when available, are announced on this page and on the News page.
