Partner with us

What we can do together

Experiments & research groups

For large international collaborations and small experiments alike, I can contribute:

  • sensor design, simulation and characterisation;
  • Geant4 detector simulation and physics-sensitivity studies;
  • timing and position reconstruction, and data analysis;
  • students and researchers based in Southeast Asia.

Industry partners

For companies developing sensors, electronics or imaging systems:

  • independent characterisation of silicon, diamond and gaseous detectors;
  • TCAD and Geant4 modelling of devices and systems;
  • prototype testing for beam, X-ray and radiation diagnostics;
  • joint grant applications and student projects.

Funding agencies & institutions

Programmes that I am well placed to lead or contribute to:

  • detector R&D and instrumentation capacity in Malaysia and the region;
  • participation in international experiments and CERN R&D;
  • medical, security and industrial applications of radiation detectors;
  • training of the next generation of experimental physicists.
