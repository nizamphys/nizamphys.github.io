Selected work

Four papers that define the research

JINST · 2024 · First author

Real-time study of radiation damage in monocrystalline diamond sensors

A thinned (~40 µm) CVD diamond sensor sampled in real time in a 67.5 MeV proton beam to ~4 × 1016 p/cm², giving a damage constant k = (3.3 ± 0.3) × 10−18 cm²/(p·µm), later confirmed with X-rays at LCLS.

Nucl. Instrum. Meth. A · 2024

Long-distance signal propagation in AC-LGAD

Measures how signals propagate along long AC-LGAD strips, a key input to large-area 4D tracking detectors.

Nuclear Physics B · 2020

Matter vs vacuum oscillations in atmospheric neutrinos

Shows that the ICAL detector can distinguish matter-modified from vacuum oscillations of atmospheric neutrinos at 3σ in ten years, for either mass ordering.

Modern Physics Letters A · 2020 · First author

Tensions between the appearance data of T2K and NOνA

Traces the disagreement between two leading long-baseline experiments on the CP phase to their electron-neutrino appearance data.
