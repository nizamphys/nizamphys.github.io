Selected work

Four papers that define the research

Nuclear Physics B · 2020 · First author

Matter vs vacuum oscillations in atmospheric neutrinos

Shows that the ICAL detector can distinguish matter-modified from vacuum oscillations of atmospheric neutrinos, for either neutrino mass ordering.

Modern Physics Letters A · 2020 · First author

Tensions between the appearance data of T2K and NOνA

Traces the disagreement between two leading long-baseline experiments on the CP phase to the electron-neutrino appearance channel.

JINST · 2024 · First author

Real-time study of radiation damage in monocrystalline diamond sensors

Follows the response of diamond sensors as radiation damage accumulates, which informs their use as beam monitors.

Nucl. Instrum. Meth. A · 2024

Long-distance signal propagation in AC-LGAD

Measures how signals travel along long AC-LGAD strips, a key input to large-area 4D tracking detectors.
