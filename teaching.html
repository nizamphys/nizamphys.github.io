Nuclear and Particle Physics

The structure of nuclei and the fundamental particles and forces, from radioactive decay to the Standard Model, and the experiments that established them.

Astrophysics and Astronomy

Supervision

Learning through research

Alongside lectures, I supervise final-year projects and postgraduate research. Students work on problems drawn directly from the group's research and learn the tools of the field: simulation, data analysis and laboratory measurement.
