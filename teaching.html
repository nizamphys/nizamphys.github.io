Teaching

Teaching

I teach courses that link classical foundations to the frontiers of modern physics, and I bring examples from current experiments into the classroom wherever I can.

Nuclear and Particle Physics

The structure of nuclei and the fundamental particles and forces, from radioactive decay to the Standard Model, and the experiments that established them.

Nuclear properties and models · radioactivity · interactions of radiation with matter · particle detectors and accelerators · quarks, leptons and symmetries · neutrino physics

Electrodynamics

The classical theory of electric and magnetic fields, building from Maxwell's equations to electromagnetic waves and radiation.

Electrostatics · magnetostatics · Maxwell's equations · electromagnetic waves · potentials and fields · radiation

Astrophysics and Astronomy

How physical laws explain the observed universe, from stars and galaxies to cosmology and the messengers, including neutrinos and cosmic rays, that carry information to us.

Observational astronomy · stellar structure and evolution · galaxies · cosmology · astroparticle physics

Supervision

Learning through research

Alongside lectures, I supervise final-year projects and postgraduate research. Students work on problems drawn directly from the group's research and learn the tools of the field: simulation, data analysis and laboratory measurement.
