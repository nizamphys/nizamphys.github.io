News

Papers, talks, student achievements and appointments from the group.

  • Feb 2027

    StudentsLiew Dek Hao selected for the ECFA Winter Instrumentation School at CERN

    Master's student Liew Dek Hao has been selected for ECFA-WISPP 2027, the first edition of the European Committee for Future Accelerators' two-week instrumentation school, held at CERN from 1 to 12 February 2027.

  • Nov 2026

    PaperNew preprint: machine learning for neutrino–antineutrino discrimination

    Liew Dek Hao's first-author paper on machine-learning-assisted ν/ν̄ discrimination in atmospheric neutrino events, and its impact on CP-violation sensitivity in a 20-kton liquid-scintillator detector, is on arXiv:2611.11474.

  • Sep 2026

    TalkGroup research presented at NuFact 2026 in Shanghai

    Liew Dek Hao presented the group's work on atmospheric neutrinos at NuFact 2026, the 27th International Workshop on Neutrinos from Accelerators, hosted by the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, Shanghai (31 August – 5 September 2026).

  • Jul 2026

    StudentsVietnam School on Neutrinos 2026

    Liew Dek Hao attended the Vietnam School on Neutrinos (VSoN 2026) at ICISE, Quy Nhon, Vietnam, 14–24 July 2026.

  • 2026

    StudentsYong Jig Tian admitted to a PhD programme at Shanghai Jiao Tong University

    Project student Yong Jig Tian, who worked on the development of Multi-gap Resistive Plate Chambers in the group, has been accepted into the doctoral programme at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

  • Apr 2025

    AppointmentAdjunct appointment at Xiamen University, China

    Dr. Nizam joined the Department of Physics of Xiamen University as Adjunct Assistant Professor.

  • Sep 2024

    PaperReal-time radiation damage in diamond sensors published in JINST

    Our study of monocrystalline CVD diamond sensors exposed to 67.5 MeV protons at the Crocker Nuclear Laboratory, UC Davis, appeared in JINST 19 (2024) P09018.

  • Feb 2024

    AppointmentJoined Xiamen University Malaysia

    Dr. Nizam joined the Department of Physics at Xiamen University Malaysia as Assistant Professor, continuing as Research Associate at SCIPP, UC Santa Cruz.
