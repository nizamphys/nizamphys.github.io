Opportunities

Join the group

I am always glad to hear from motivated students who want to work on real physics problems and real detectors. Students in the group take part in the full research cycle, from simulation and hardware to analysis and publication, and can connect with international collaborators.

PhD

Doctoral projects in neutrino phenomenology and analysis, or in detector development: LGAD and diamond sensors, gaseous detectors, and diagnostics. Strong physics fundamentals and programming experience are valued.

Master's by research

Focused one- to two-year projects, for example machine-learning analysis of neutrino events, Geant4 or TCAD simulation, or characterisation of detector prototypes.

Final-year & project students

Undergraduate projects for physics students at Xiamen University Malaysia, designed to give a genuine first research experience with simulation, data or hardware.

Internships & visiting students

Short research stays for students from other universities, when a suitable project and supervision are available.

How to apply

Getting in touch

Please email mohammad.nizam@xmu.edu.my with the subject line “Prospective student – [PhD / Master's / Project / Internship]”, and include:

  • a CV and academic transcripts;
  • a short paragraph on which research theme interests you and why;
  • any programming, laboratory or research experience;
  • your intended start date.

Formal admission to postgraduate programmes is through Xiamen University Malaysia. Funded positions, when available, are announced on this page.
