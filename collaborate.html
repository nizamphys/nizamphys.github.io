Current collaborations

Where the work happens

CERN · Geneva

DRD3 Collaboration

CERN's detector R&D collaboration on solid-state detectors, developing the silicon and diamond sensor technologies of future experiments.

UC Santa Cruz · USA

Santa Cruz Institute for Particle Physics

Continuing research on ultra-fast silicon detectors, AC-LGADs and diamond sensors as a Research Associate.

Hyderabad · India

TIFR Hyderabad

Collaboration with the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Hyderabad.

Shanghai · China

Tsung-Dao Lee Institute

Collaboration with TDLI on neutrino physics and detector research.

Xiamen · China

Xiamen University

Adjunct appointment linking the Malaysia and China campuses in teaching and research.

Professional societies

AAPPS-DPP & PSSI

Partner with us

What we can do together

Experiments & research groups

For large international collaborations and small experiments alike:

  • sensor design, simulation and characterisation;
  • Geant4 detector simulation and physics-sensitivity studies;
  • timing and position reconstruction and data analysis;
  • students and researchers based in Southeast Asia.

Industry partners

For companies developing sensors, electronics or imaging systems:

  • characterisation of silicon, diamond and gaseous detectors;
  • TCAD, Geant4 and finite-element modelling of devices and systems;
  • prototype testing for beam, X-ray and radiation diagnostics;
  • joint grant applications and student projects.

Funding agencies & institutions

Programmes I am well placed to lead or contribute to:

  • detector R&D and instrumentation capacity in Malaysia and the region;
  • participation in international experiments and CERN R&D;
  • medical, security and industrial uses of radiation detectors;
  • training the next generation of experimental physicists.
