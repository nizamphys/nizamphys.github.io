01 — About

From atmospheric neutrinos to picosecond timing

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at Xiamen University Malaysia (since 2024). My work sits where theory meets hardware: I use precision simulation and data analysis to ask what neutrino experiments can learn, and I develop and characterise the detectors that make those measurements possible.

I earned my PhD in High Energy Physics from Homi Bhabha National Institute (2020), working at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research on the physics potential of the magnetised Iron Calorimeter (ICAL) proposed for the India-based Neutrino Observatory (INO) — from hadron energy reconstruction in Geant4 to the discrimination of matter-modified versus vacuum oscillations and the tension between T2K and NOνA data.

From 2020 to 2024 I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Santa Cruz Institute for Particle Physics (SCIPP), UC Santa Cruz, one of the leading groups in ultra-fast silicon detectors. There I worked on AC-coupled Low Gain Avalanche Diodes, electronic-grade diamond sensors for extreme environments, X-ray detection at synchrotron light sources, front-end ASIC testing, and beam diagnostics for the Isotope Production Facility at Los Alamos National Laboratory.

I enjoy teaching physics, mentoring students into research, and building collaborations across particle physics, accelerator science and medical physics. Prospective students and collaborators are welcome to get in touch.

02 — Research

Two pillars, one question: what are neutrinos telling us?

Particle & neutrino physics

Phenomenology and simulation for atmospheric and long-baseline neutrino experiments, and the open questions of the Standard Model.

  • Neutrino mass hierarchy (ordering)
  • Absolute neutrino mass measurement
  • Matter effects in neutrino oscillations
  • CP violation in the lepton sector
  • Neutrinoless double beta decay
  • Dark matter searches

Instrumentation & simulation

Development, simulation and testing of fast, precise and radiation-hard detectors — and their transfer to applications beyond particle physics.

  • Low Gain Avalanche Diodes (LGADs, AC-LGADs)
  • Diamond-based sensors
  • Resistive Plate Chambers & MRPCs
  • Time Projection Chambers
  • Accelerator beam diagnostics & DAQ systems
  • Medical imaging, hadron therapy, muon tomography

03 — Selected projects

Research experience

SCIPP · UC Santa Cruz

Electronic-grade diamond sensors for extreme environments

Characterising single-crystal diamond detectors as high-flux, high-rate, radiation-hard pass-through diagnostics for synchrotron and next-generation X-ray free-electron laser beams.

  • Diamond
  • X-ray FEL
  • Beam diagnostics

SCIPP · UC Santa Cruz

TCAD simulation of charge collection in LGADs & diamond

Device-level TCAD modelling of gain, charge collection and signal formation in LGAD and diamond sensors, validated against laser TCT and test-beam measurements.

  • TCAD
  • LGAD
  • Signal formation

SCIPP · UC Santa Cruz

AC-LGADs for 4D tracking

Signal sharing and long-distance signal propagation in AC-coupled LGADs (resistive silicon detectors) with strip readout — studying the roles of strip length, pitch, N+ resistivity and coupling for future 4D trackers such as those at the Electron-Ion Collider.

  • AC-LGAD
  • Laser TCT
  • Test beam

Los Alamos National Laboratory

SWIM — Submerged Wireless Imaging Monitor

A submerged, wireless beam-imaging monitor for beam diagnostics at the Isotope Production Facility, enabling in-situ monitoring of the proton beam that produces medical isotopes.

  • Accelerator
  • Isotopes
  • Imaging

TIFR · INO

Multi-gap RPCs for Time-of-Flight PET

Feasibility study of six-gap Multi-gap Resistive Plate Chambers as a low-cost, large-area, fast-timing alternative to crystals for time-of-flight Positron Emission Tomography.

  • MRPC
  • TOF-PET
  • Medical physics

TIFR · INO-ICAL

Hadron energy reconstruction in atmospheric neutrino events

Geant4 simulation of hadron showers in the 50 kton magnetised Iron Calorimeter and a procedure to estimate hadron energy from hit multiplicity — improving neutrino energy reconstruction for oscillation analyses.

  • Geant4
  • ICAL
  • Calorimetry
